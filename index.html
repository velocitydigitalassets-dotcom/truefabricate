Fabrication Terminology Matrix

Bend Allowance (BA) The exact length of material needed to navigate a bend. Accurately determining BA prevents stretching or cracking during brake forming operations.
K-Factor Value The ratio shifting the neutral axis position relative to sheet metal material thickness during a mechanical sheet bend operation.
Metallurgical Density The raw volume-to-mass weight ratio. Measured across alloy groups including carbon structural steels, aluminum plates, and copper flats.
